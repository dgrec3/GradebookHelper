Rows & colors

Lower strength gives softer colors and lets PowerTeacher's gray row stripes show through colored cells, so each student's row stays easy to follow.

Lighter Darker

Assignment highlights

Bands go from lowest to highest. The first band starts at 0%; type where each band ends, and the next band starts there automatically (a score of exactly 70 belongs to the band that starts at 70). Leave the last band's end blank for "and up". Uncheck a band to leave its range uncolored.

Final grade

Score codes

Color cells whose score or final grade is a special code from your district's grade scale (for example M for Medical). A code's color wins over the score bands.

Flags

L M

Each flag always sits in the same spot on the cell's left edge: M (missing) top-left, I (incomplete) top-right, L (late) bottom-left, A (absent) bottom-right. E (exempt) sits on its own. Flags don't change the cell's color.
