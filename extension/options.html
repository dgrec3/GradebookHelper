PowerSchool Enhancer

Grade highlighting for PowerTeacher Pro scoresheets

Assignment highlights

A score is in a band when it is at least Min and below Max. Leave Max blank for "and above". The first matching band from the top wins.

Final grade

Score codes

Color cells whose score or final grade is a special code from your district's grade scale (for example M for Medical). Codes override flags and score bands.

Flags

Color cells that PowerTeacher has flagged. A flag color overrides the score band. If a cell has several flags, the highest one in this list wins.
