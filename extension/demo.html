Gradebook Enhancer demo

A sample PowerTeacher Pro scoresheet with made-up students

This page runs the same highlighting code that runs on your PowerTeacher Pro scoresheet, using your saved settings. Change something in Settings and this table updates right away. No real student data is used.

Flags, score codes and the final grade column are off by default. Turn them on in Settings, or use the button below to switch on every highlight type at once.

Student

Flag tags: L = late, M = missing, I = incomplete, A = absent, E = exempt (full words can be chosen in Settings). Turn off "text tags" in Settings to see PowerTeacher's usual colored squares instead.
